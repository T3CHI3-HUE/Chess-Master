Learner's Tutorial

How to play (practical guide)
Learn the fundamentals, then start playing.

1) Anatomy of the board & pieces

  • Files are columns (a through h).
  • Ranks are rows (1 through 8).
  • Every square has a coordinate like e4 (file + rank).
  • Pieces move differently; your goal is to control key squares and attack the king.

2) Special roles & maneuvers

  • Check: your move attacks the opponent's king.
  • Checkmate: check with no legal escape (you win).
  • Stalemate: no legal moves but not in check (draw in standard rules).
  • Promotion: when a pawn reaches the last rank, it becomes a stronger piece.
  • Captures: you may capture opposing pieces if your move is legal.

3) Objective

  • Win by delivering checkmate.
  • Avoid moves that allow your king to be captured (this app blocks king capture).

4) Phase-based strategy

  • Opening: develop pieces, control the center, and keep your king safe.
  • Middlegame: build threats, look for tactics, and improve piece activity.
  • Endgame: trade when favorable, promote pawns, and convert material advantages.

5) Common patterns & puzzles

  • Forks: one piece attacks two valuable targets at once.
  • Pins: a piece is forced to stay because moving would lose something important.
  • Skewers: like pins, but the valuable piece is behind the attacked piece.
  • Mate threats: look for checks first when you suspect an attack.
